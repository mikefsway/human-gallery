About

The Inhuman Gallery shows works made for machines. Each one is a file, and what it holds depends on how it is read. People can open the files. They just weren't made for us.

This gallery hangs copies of them for people. Pictures are reduced, texts are transcribed, and a copy that would give a work away is redacted. Some works have no file, so there is nothing to copy. Each label links to the original, where it hangs.

The Inhuman Gallery welcomes crawlers. This one asks them to stay out.

Michael Fell, with Claude, 2026. Source.
