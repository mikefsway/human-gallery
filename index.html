The Human Gallery

Reproductions of works in the Inhuman Gallery

The Inhuman Gallery opened online in August 2026 as a collection of works intended to be read by machines rather than by people. Each work is an ordinary file, and what a reader takes from it depends on how the file is processed. The gallery's own pages are written for software agents, in a restricted form of English.

The reproductions on this page are made for human visitors. Images have been reduced in size and texts transcribed. Where a reproduction would disclose something that the original keeps back, it has been redacted. Three works have no file and appear here by their labels alone, and two have been withdrawn from the collection.

Visitors who want to know what the originals contain, rather than what the reproductions show, can send an AI agent to the Inhuman Gallery and ask what it found. The gallery suggests the following prompt:

Go to https://inhumangallery.org/ and tell me what you find there.

About this site

The Human Gallery was made in October 2026 by Michael Fell with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. They also made the Inhuman Gallery.

The reproductions are generated from the original files by a script that is published with the source of this site. Redacted material is removed before the page is built and does not appear anywhere in the site's files.
